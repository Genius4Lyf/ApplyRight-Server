Responsibilities:Financial Management & Reporting:

	• Daily
Reconciliation: Perform daily cash, POS, and bank transfer reconciliations for
clinic transactions, ensuring zero discrepancy between the Electronic Medical
Record (EMR) system billing records and bank statements.
	• Receivables
& Debt Recovery: Actively track outstanding payments from HMOs and
corporate partners. Investigate unresolved or disputed / rejected claims
promptly to maintain a healthy Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) metric.
	• Accounts
Payable: Process payments for vendors, suppliers, and utility bills, ensuring
all expenses are properly authorized, receipted, and categorized.
	• Financial
Reporting: Prepare monthly cash flow summaries and monthly Profit & Loss
(P&L) statements.
	• Payroll
& Compliance: Assist in monthly payroll preparation. Manage statutory tax
deductions and remittances (PAYE, Pension, WHT, NHF etc) in line with
state and
federal regulations.
	• Audit
Support: Maintain clean, audit-ready physical and digital financial
archives to
support end-of-year internal and external audits.

Clinic Administration & Facility
Management:

	• Inventory
& Procurement Control: Monitor and manage inventory levels for clinic
consumables (drugs, stationeries, and cleaning supplies etc).
	• Asset
Registers: Maintain fixed asset registers and conduct periodic inspections.
	• Regulatory
& admin Support: Track and coordinate renewal dates for state and local
business permits and local health authority clinical certifications (e.g.
HEFAMAA), leases etc.
	• HR
Admin Support: Monitor staff attendance & off days.
